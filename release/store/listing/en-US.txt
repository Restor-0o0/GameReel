Название (title):
GameReel

Краткое описание (short description, до 80 символов):
Generate game ideas with mechanics, settings, combinations and reel presets.

Полное описание (full description):
Find a starting point for your next game. Spin the reels to combine mechanics, visual styles, constraints and settings. Keep promising components and reroll the rest.

• Tag libraries with explanations and an editorial selection of game references.
• Customizable reels and saved presets.
• Suggestions for compatible mechanics and rare pairs within selected libraries.
• Idea of the day: one combination per day, the same for everyone.
• Share an idea as a link with friends or your team.
• Saved ideas and notes with prototype, game jam and released statuses and a result link.
• Game packs that turn the reels into a randomizer, such as a hero, items and talents for Dota 2.
• Import your own JSON libraries and presets, community catalog on GitHub.
• 14 interface languages, animation and sound settings.

Core features work offline without an account. Source links open in your browser.
Rarity reflects the selected annotated libraries; it does not prove an idea is unique.
GameReel is an independent tool and is not affiliated with the referenced games, publishers or stores.
